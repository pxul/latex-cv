\roottitle{Career Development}

\headedsection{USS Enterprise (NCC-1701-D) (\textit{6 years})}{Galaxy-class starship}{
  \headedsubsection{Second Officer and Chief Operations Officer}{2364 -- present}{
    \bodytext{Third in the chain of command, under Captain Jean-Luc Picard. Responsible for the allocation of the ship's resources and the coordination of requests between departments, from the operations console on the bridge.
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Rights:} Was the subject of a hearing before the Judge Advocate General in 2365, to determine whether I am the property of Starfleet and may be disassembled for study. The ruling, that I have the right to choose, was made over the arguments of the ship's first officer, who had been ordered to argue against me and did so well. We remain friends.
      \item \textbf{Discretion:} Have on one occasion been ordered by my captain to conceal the events of an entire day from the crew, including from the captain himself. I complied, under questioning from all of them.
      \item \textbf{Resilience:} Was held for a period as an exhibit in the private collection of the trader Kivas Fajo, who had staged my death in a shuttle explosion. I declined to sit in the chair provided. On my recovery, the transporter chief reported that my weapon had been in a state of discharge as I was beamed out. Perhaps something occurred during transport.
    \end{itemize}}
  }
}

\headedsection{USS Sutherland (\textit{temporary command})}{Klingon--Romulan border}{
  \headedsubsection{Commanding Officer}{2368}{
    \bodytext{Commanded a Nebula-class starship in the blockade that prevented Romulan supplies from reaching the Duras family during the Klingon civil war. Disobeyed a direct order to rejoin the fleet, in order to expose a cloaked Romulan convoy. Captain Picard observed afterwards that the claim to have been following orders has been used to justify too many tragedies; I have taken this as approval. My first officer expressed reservations about serving under an android. I do not believe they were resolved, but the convoy was.}
  }
}
